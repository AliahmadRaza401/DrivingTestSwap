Delete Your DriveSwap Account

How to permanently delete your account and associated data.

DriveSwap lets you request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. You can do this directly inside the app, or by contacting us.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the DriveSwap app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm on the dialog. Your account is then permanently deleted.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address linked to your account at:

Shadow.drive369@gmail.com

Use the subject line Delete my account. We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account credentials and login (email/password).
  • Your profile details (name, username, date of birth).
  • Your posts, swap history, and payment records associated with your account.
  • Your messages and conversations.

Data we may retain

We may retain certain information where required by law (for example, transaction records for tax or accounting purposes) for the legally required period, after which it is deleted. Retained data is not used for any other purpose.
